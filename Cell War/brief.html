CELLWARS

A mass arena on a phosphor grid. You and the bots start at 100. Eat food and smaller cells, split to hunt, and hold merge to pull yourself back together. Clear the field, or get eaten.

MASS

EAT · SPLIT · MERGE · EJECT

Mass is the score, summed across every piece you own. Bigger cells move slower. The HUD shows your rank and that total. The board lists the top 10, and pins you underneath if you fall out of it.

  • EAT

    Cover them

    You swallow a cell only when your mass is strictly above 1.25 times theirs, and your body covers most of theirs. Their mass is added to yours. Pieces you own cannot eat each other.

  • SPLIT

    From 72

    A piece of at least 72 mass halves and throws the new half toward your aim. Both pieces lock split for 1 second and merge for 2. Every ready piece splits together.

  • MERGE

    Hold it

    After the 2 second lock, holding merge pulls your pieces into the largest one. They combine when they overlap. Bots merge on their own. You have to hold.

  • EJECT

    Drop 10

    Your largest piece can drop 10 mass as a pellet when it still has at least 24. You cannot eat your own pellet for under a second. Anyone else can. Bots do not eject.

FIELD

FOOD · VIRUSES · PICKUPS

Food pellets are 1–3 mass. The field refills to your food setting, and ejected pellets sit on top of that count. Five viruses, each 100 mass, rest near the corners and the center. You cannot eat a virus.

VIRUS

Touch one with more than 115 mass and you burst into 4 to 16 equal pieces. A piece still inside its 1 second split lock will not burst. Otherwise the virus only shoves you off.

PICKUPS

Up to three on the field. One is there when the run starts, and another tries to appear every 15 seconds while there is room. Bots can take them. A pickup applies to every piece you own.

  • SPEED 1.9× move speed for 11 seconds
  • SHIELD One hit. The cell that would eat you is shoved off, and the shield breaks.
  • MAGNET Pulls food toward your largest piece for 10 seconds

RUN

LOSS · VICTORY · RETRY

LOSS

Lose every piece and the run ends. The arena keeps moving. The camera follows the bot that ate you, or the largest bot if that one is already gone.

VICTORY

Eat every bot — every piece of them — and the run is Dominion Complete. Two cell eats inside four seconds show a combo. A short feed records when you eat, or get eaten.

RETRY

Retry drops you back into the same field at 100 mass. Bots and food stay. Play Again after a win builds a new field. Best on the end screen is the peak mass of this life.

CONTROLS

MOUSE · KEYS · PAD

The mouse aims. The camera leads a little toward the cursor. The map starts on: you, the other cells, and the five viruses. M toggles it. Your name takes up to 12 characters.

  • SPACE Split toward the cursor
  • SHIFT Hold to merge
  • W Eject from your largest piece
  • M Toggle the map
  • S Split
  • U Merge
  • E Eject
  • M Map

[ TABLET ] On a touch tablet whose shorter side is at least 600 pixels, the stick aims and sets your speed — push it farther to move faster, up to full speed. Hold S to keep splitting, U to merge, E to keep ejecting. M toggles the map. Same rules.

Options set zoom (72% default), camera smoothing, bots from 10 to 40, food from 400 to 1200, sound, and your color. Cyan is the default. Save applies them until you reload.

Grow, or get eaten. The grid keeps both.
