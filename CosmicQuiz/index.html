ABOUT

COSMIC QUIZ is a run of fifty questions about space, technology, and the strange facts that sit between them. No capitals. No maps. Open it alone, or pass a room code to one friend.

MODES

CLASSIC — A wrong answer costs a life. The next question follows.

SURVIVAL — A miss costs a life and keeps this question until you answer it.

BLITZ — Fifteen seconds each. Faster answers score higher. Running out of time costs a life.

SPACEJUNK GAMES makes small games for the quiet between bigger ones. The alien above is the same mark as Nebula Void: a signal, a blink, then you are on your own.
