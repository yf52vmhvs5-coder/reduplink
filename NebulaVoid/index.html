NEBULA VOID is a top-down survival arena set in a drifting starfield around a hungry singularity. You are not a hero — you are an intruder: a living cell fighting to grow before the void pulls you in.

Consume stardust and nutrients, hunt smaller entities, and dodge anything bigger than you. Chain kills to raise your bounty multiplier. Each run earns Void Matter for unlocking new skins and tuning them in the Evolution Lab.

Three playable entities ship with distinct powers: blood rifts, warp freezes, and corrosive dashes. The map is alive with rival bots — titans, glitches, nebulae — each with its own temperament.
